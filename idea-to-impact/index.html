Idea to Impact

Bring us a problem or opportunity. Sixteen working hours later, your users test one workflow from it.

Bring a problem or an opportunity that your organisation wants solved. Behind it sits a value stream of workflows. We map that value stream, build one workflow from it, and your users test it. Four of the sixteen hours are your people's time.

The clock runs for each step

Each step gives the next step a finished artefact.

The result is a complete trace from the sponsor's problem to a workflow that your users have tested. Nobody has to assemble the trace afterwards.

A number with a ring is a step that needs people from your organisation. There are four such steps.

Your stakeholder time

4:00h

Only four of the sixteen hours need time from your organisation: the sponsor session, the observation session, the review, and the confirmation. The other twelve hours run without your people.

The clock assumes one observation session. No step in the list is a status meeting, a handover, or a document that updates an old record.

Start with one workflow.

Step one is a one-hour session with your sponsor. Every later step runs from what you agree in that session.
