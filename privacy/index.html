Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy tells you what personal data Fusion collects when you use fusionframe.work, why we collect it, and how you can control it. We follow the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore (PDPA).

Last updated: 16 September 2026

  • This website sets no cookies.
  • We do not follow you to other websites.
  • We show no advertising.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.

Who we are

Fusion operates fusionframe.work and controls the personal data that this policy describes. Send questions, requests and complaints about your personal data to our Data Protection Officer at hello@fusionframe.work.

Website analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to count visits to this website. Plausible does not use cookies, does not store your IP address, and does not identify you. Plausible cannot follow you from one website to another, or from one day to the next.

Plausible records these facts about each visit:

  • The page you visit, and the website that sent you, if any
  • Your browser, operating system and type of device
  • Your country, region and city, taken from your IP address. Plausible then discards the IP address.
  • Clicks on some links, for example the link that books a meeting

We see only totals, for example the number of visitors to a page in one week. We use the totals to learn which pages are useful and to improve the website. Plausible stores the data on servers in the European Union.

When you book a meeting

The “Book a demo” and booking links open a Google Calendar booking page. To book, you give your name, your email address, and any notes you choose to add. Google processes that data under the Google Privacy Policy, and Google sends the booking to us.

We use your booking details to hold the meeting, to send you material that you ask for, and to follow up about Fusion. We do not add you to a mailing list.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name, and the content of your message. We use them to reply to you and to continue the conversation.

Files from other servers

To show the website, your browser downloads some files from other providers. Each provider receives your IP address and browser details, because it must have them to send the file. We do not receive that data.

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) sends the typefaces.
  • unpkg (unpkg.com) sends the script that draws the icons.
  • Simple Icons (cdn.simpleicons.org) sends the technology logos.

Transfers outside Singapore

Our providers store and process data outside Singapore. Plausible uses servers in the European Union. Google uses servers in many countries. We use only providers that protect personal data to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

How long we keep data

  • Analytics totals: while we use Plausible. The totals contain no personal data.
  • Bookings and emails: while we work with you or talk with you about Fusion, and for up to two years after our last contact. If the law requires us to keep a record for longer, we keep it for that period.

Your rights

Under the PDPA, you can ask us to do these things:

  • Tell you what personal data about you we have, and how we used it in the last year
  • Correct personal data about you that is wrong or incomplete
  • Stop the use of your personal data. If you withdraw your consent, we tell you what the result is before we act.
  • Delete your booking details and your emails

Email your request to hello@fusionframe.work. We can ask you to confirm who you are. We reply within 30 days. If we need more time, we tell you when to expect the reply. We do not charge for a correction. If an access request needs a large amount of work, we can charge a reasonable fee, and we tell you the fee before we start.

How we protect data

The website uses HTTPS for all traffic. We keep booking details and emails in accounts that only our team can open. If a data breach puts your personal data at risk, we tell you and the Personal Data Protection Commission as the PDPA requires.

Complaints

If you have a concern, email hello@fusionframe.work first, so that we can try to fix it. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal data, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we also tell the people who booked a meeting or emailed us.
