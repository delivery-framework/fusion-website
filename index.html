Build or buy

Three traditional ways to get business software. Now there is a better way.

If you build it, you wait a year for something that fits. If you buy a package, you have something next month that does not fit. If you build on a low-code platform, you come closer to a fit, until you meet the platform's ceiling. You also work under the vendor's licence, with your logic in their format.

Fusion takes the finished plumbing and the fast start from the products. Fusion takes the fit, the ownership and the freedom from the self-build option.
