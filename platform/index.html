Analytics

Get an answer without a fight.

The application already holds the data that can answer every question anyone asks about your business. To get the answer is the part that takes three weeks. Those weeks go on a request to a data team, an extract, a spreadsheet, and a number that nobody fully trusts. Fusion puts the analysis inside the application, where the data already is and where the permission rules already are.

Notebooks, not a second product

Reactive notebooks run in the browser, inside the application. Change one input, and every chart and table below it recalculates. There is no separate reporting product with its own logins, its own nightly copy of your data, and its own bill for each analyst.

Describe the question in plain words

The assistant writes the query and draws the chart. Every line of it stays visible and editable. Nobody waits three weeks in a queue behind the one person who knows the reporting tool.

The notebook shows only what that person can already see

Every query runs through the same permission rules as every screen in the application. There is no second copy of your access rules that someone keeps in step by hand. A second copy is how somebody sees a number they were never meant to see.

Why this matters more than you expect

The usual approach is a reporting tool that points at a nightly copy of the database, with your permission rules rebuilt inside it by hand. You then maintain two sets of rules that must agree forever. The first time they silently disagree, somebody sees a salary, a case note or a client they had no right to see. The software tells nobody.

AI in the application

AI is a first-class citizen.

The AI is not a chatbot added after go-live. The AI in a Fusion application holds the same objects, permissions and workflows as the people who use it. So the AI can do the work, not only talk about it.

Real tool calls

The AI acts through the same governed actions as the screens: create the record, advance the workflow, run the report. Fusion checks the permissions on every call, and every call lands in the audit trail like the actions of any other user.

AI services where the work is

Purpose-built AI services attach to specific workflows: triage the intake queue, draft the letter, reconcile the accounts. We specify and test them like any other part of the application.

Voice instead of screens

Conversational voice AI carries whole tasks, so hands stay free and screens stay closed. Say what happened, and the record, the workflow and the audit trail update as if you had typed it.

Video conferencing that knows the task

Conferencing runs on the platform itself, with task-aware and context-aware AI in the call. The AI joins with the record open, captures the decisions, and turns them into actions.

Custom interfaces

The back office can look like a business app. What your customers touch cannot.

A handful of people use most screens in a business application, and they want the screens fast, consistent and unobtrusive. Thousands of customers use a few screens, and those screens must be designed, not generated. Fusion does not ask you to choose between the two. Fusion does not make you start again to get from one to the other.

01 no code at all

Leave it generated

For the screens that twelve people use for their daily work, a generated screen is the right answer. A generated screen is consistent and complete, and it follows the model without maintenance by anybody.

02 one file

Replace one piece of it

Put your own version in the ordinary place, and it wins. You design one table, one row or one form section, and everything around it stays generated.

03 one file, everywhere

Restyle the whole application

Fusion builds the interface from a small set of shared pieces. Redraw the button once, and every button in the application changes with it, in every theme.

04 your screen, your code

Build it yourself

When a screen must be a designed thing, not only a competent one, take it and build it. You waste nothing. The generated screen was only a placeholder until then.

Skills that AI does well, and that you can hire for

A custom Fusion screen is ordinary web work, the most widely written code there is. That fact helps you twice. AI assistants are strongest at ordinary web work, because they were trained on more of it than anything else. Any front-end developer or design agency already knows ordinary web work, so you can hire in an open market. A rented platform's own interface framework is the reverse on both counts. Far fewer people write it, so AI is weaker at it, and far fewer people know it, so the specialists who do charge more.
