The method

Hours of your people's time, not weeks of workshops.

Three of the six steps need people from the business. The other three steps run without them.

A ringed number shows a step that needs people from the business. There are three such steps.

  1. 01

    Problem & Opportunity Statement session

    1 hour Business time

    We meet the sponsor. We agree the problem to solve, the opportunity, the success measures and the platform system in scope.

    Session transcript Problem & Opportunity Statement
  2. 02

    Observation sessions

    1–2 hours each Business time

    The people who use the system today show us their work. We record the pain points, the workarounds and the work that happens outside the platform.

    Observation notes
  3. 03

    Metadata analysis

    We read the eight parts of the system from the platform's metadata. We compare them with the observation notes, to find the parts that people use and the parts that nobody uses.

    Current State System Architecture Current State Workflows
  4. 04

    Domain model and design

    We design the domain model and the workflows on Fusion. Each pain point has a fix, and each obligation has a control.

    Domain Model Workflows Value Stream
  5. 05

    Build, test and document

    Business time

    Fusion builds each workflow through the five lifecycle gates: spec, build, system testing, user guide and user testing. The people who use the workflow test it before it goes live.

    System tests User Guide User Guide Video
  6. 06

    Data migration and switch-over

    We move your records into your PostgreSQL database and deploy the new system to production. Then you can end your Salesforce, ServiceNow or Dynamics 365 subscription.

    Production deploy
