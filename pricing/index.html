Any number of people can use the software you build: ten, ten thousand, or everyone you serve. None of them is licensed. The seats above are for the few people who build with Fusion. That rule is the full difference between Fusion and a platform that charges you for each person who logs in.

On this page, “no per-seat licensing” has one meaning. The seats above licence the few people who build with Fusion. We never charge anybody to use what they built. Growth in your user base is not a pricing event.

List prices are per year. We set prices in Australian dollars. The US dollar figures are rounded equivalents, not a live conversion, and the agreement fixes the rate. We quote delivery services for each engagement. The amount you pay depends on how many environments you run and how many people build with Fusion. Treat these prices as the basis of a quote, not the quote itself.

How the replacement deal works

No lock-in

Here is exactly what it costs you to leave.

Every platform claims there is no lock-in. Almost none of them will tell you what an exit involves. So here is the honest ledger, before you sign, not after.

You keep

  • Your documents

    Your documents are the discovery, the design decisions, and the delivery record. They are plain files in your repository. They stay readable with or without us.

  • Your business logic

    Your business logic is the models and the rules that describe how your organisation works. This part is the result of the design work, and the code is ordinary Rails that any Rails developer can maintain.

  • Your database, and everything in it

    You keep a standard PostgreSQL database with a standard schema. The database is not a file export that you must reassemble. You keep the live database, in operation, with your data in it.

You must replace

  • The interface

    Fusion generates your screens. If you remove Fusion, you also remove the screens, so you must build your own. This rebuild is the real cost of an exit, and we do not pretend it is small.

  • The AI-assisted pipeline

    The pipeline is the path from discovery to delivery, and the conventions that keep AI output as reviewable work. You return to the process you used before.

The exit is a practical route out, not a full system replacement. You rebuild an interface, not a system.

Now compare it with an exit from a rented platform

Your data leaves as a file export that somebody must reassemble into a system that works. You wrote your business rules in a language that runs nowhere else. Your screens were configuration inside a product that you can no longer access. There is no route out, practical or otherwise. The lock-in is not a design error. The lock-in is the commercial model.

Decide with software that works, not a proposal.
