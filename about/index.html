About Fusion

AI made software fast. We built Fusion so you can trust it.

AI can now write software faster than anyone can check it. So the real question is not speed. The question is trust: can you trust what was built, and can you prove it to your auditors, your board and your users?

Why we built Fusion

Delivery lost its connection to the business.

Twenty years of “Big Agile” pulled delivery away from the business. Teams turned the work into tickets, but lost the connection to the business process and the domain design. Poor design needed more and more architectural complexity. Now AI adds code faster on top of that complexity.

We took a different route. With the introduction of AI, we went back to first principles and transformed how working enterprise software is delivered and maintained. The result is a significant improvement in quality and maintainability, in agility and cost, in stakeholder engagement and user feedback loops, and in security, governance and compliance.

Our mission

Radically improve how enterprise software is delivered and maintained, and return system sovereignty to our customers.

System sovereignty means that your data, your schema and your business logic are yours, on the hosting that you choose.

What we believe

01

The big SaaS platforms are now legacy.

Salesforce, ServiceNow and Microsoft Dynamics were built before AI, to solve a different set of problems. Their proprietary, multi-tenant architectures were built for “clicks, not code”, and they are now 20 to 30 years old.

02

No tollgate on your own data.

You already pay for your LLMs. You should not have to pay a tollgate fee each time you use them to access your own data and your own business logic.

The Fusion SDLC

The discipline, back in every change

The lifecycle is built into the work: the roadmap, the workflow gates, the specs, system testing and user testing. AI makes the full SDLC affordable on every change, not only on the projects with the largest budgets. Each artefact traces to the one before it, so the audit trail is a result of the work, not a rush before each release.

See the lifecycle

The Fusion Platform

Built for AI, not for clicks

The platform gives you what low-code platforms promise: records, workflows, screens and analytics, ready to use. But we built it from the start for AI in the delivery pipeline, on open-source foundations. It does not carry decades of proprietary architecture built for “clicks, not code”. The AI writes about 90% less code, and the result is better performance, agility and maintainability.

See the platform

Who we are

A small, senior team.

The team is small because the Fusion Platform provides much of what a larger team builds by hand: the screens, the permissions, the audit and the API. The Fusion SDLC uses AI for work that needed specialist roles before: business analysis, development, testing and deployment. A named person reviews every artefact.
